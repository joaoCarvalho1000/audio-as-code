Give it to your agent

Give your project a soundtrack. A tiny dragon's boss fight. A quarterly report with a villain arc. A launch video with a pulse. Describe the scene; your agent composes the music and delivers a WAV you can use. You can change the score whenever the scene changes.

  2. Copy the prompt

  3. Paste it in, then listen

    Your agent writes and renders the music, then places the WAV in your creative project. Keep the whole composition for the next revision:

    • WAV ready for your project
    • MIDI notes for your DAW
    • score.json the editable score
    • source the composition code
    • report duration and level checks
